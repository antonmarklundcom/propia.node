# Legal and tax questions for the new guides — research prompt

The founder pastes the prompt below into Gemini Deep Research, then pastes the
answer back as `docs/research/legal-answers-2026-10-01.md`. Guides B1–B7 use
only answers that come with an official source; everything else stays a
question to ask a notary or lawyer. The answers are research, not legal
advice — the guides say so.

---

```text
Research Paraguayan law and current practice (2025-2026) for a real-estate website's consumer guides. For EVERY answer give: the rule in one or two plain sentences, the exact source (law number and article, decree, or official government page) with a link, the date of the source, and a confidence level. Prefer primary sources: bacn.gov.py (laws), the Corte Suprema de Justicia, the Dirección General de los Registros Públicos, the Servicio Nacional de Catastro, the DNIT (tax authority), municipal websites (Asunción). Mark clearly where practice differs from the written law, where a rule changed recently, and where you could not find a primary source. Do not guess numbers.

A. Residential and commercial leases (locación / arrendamiento)
1. Which law governs leases of homes and of commercial premises in Paraguay (Código Civil articles, any special lease law)? Is "arrendamiento" legally different from "locación" or "alquiler"?
2. Must a lease be in writing? Must it be signed before a notary or registered anywhere to be valid or enforceable against third parties?
3. Minimum and maximum lease terms set by law. What happens when a lease ends and the tenant stays (tácita reconducción)?
4. Security deposit (depósito en garantía): any legal maximum, rules for returning it, customary amount in practice.
5. Rent increases: are indexation or annual adjustment clauses allowed (in guaraníes or US dollars)? Any legal cap?
6. Rent in US dollars: allowed? Any rule on paying in guaraníes at the exchange rate?
7. Early termination by the tenant and by the landlord: legal notice periods and penalties.
8. Who pays repairs, municipal taxes, expensas (building fees) and utilities by law, and what is customary?
9. Eviction (desalojo): grounds and a plain outline of the procedure and typical duration.
10. Taxes on rental income for an individual landlord (IRP, IVA): when they apply and whether the tenant needs a factura.
11. Guarantees used in Paraguay: garantía propietaria (a guarantor who owns property), fianza, seguro de caución, cash deposit. What each legally implies for the guarantor.
12. Lease with an option to buy (alquiler con opción a compra): is there specific regulation? What must the contract fix?
13. Rural leases (campo para siembra, ganadería): any special rules or terms.

B. Buying property
14. Título de propiedad vs. escritura pública: what each is in Paraguay, and how a buyer verifies a title and its liens (Registro Público: certificado de condiciones de dominio, informe de gravámenes) — steps and official links.
15. Catastro: what the cuenta corriente catastral is, how to look it up online at the Servicio Nacional de Catastro (exact official URL), and what the lookup shows.
16. Costs of a purchase: notary fees (is there an official arancel for escribanos?), registry fees, any transfer tax, IVA or IRP on the sale, and who customarily pays each (buyer or seller).
17. Impuesto inmobiliario: who levies it (municipality), the tax base (valor fiscal), the rate range set by law, when and where it is paid, and whether a buyer inherits unpaid tax.
18. Foreign buyers: restrictions on owning land (e.g. the border-security strip law), and whether a foreigner needs a cédula or RUC to buy.
19. Buying land without a title (derechos, posesión): the legal risk and how titling works.
20. Mortgage loans for housing: the legal requirements of a mortgage (hipoteca) and its registration; any public programmes for first homes (AFD, MUVH) with official links — no rates unless officially published, with date.

C. Gated communities (barrios cerrados)
21. The legal form of a barrio cerrado in Paraguay (condominium, propiedad horizontal, private subdivision), and what a buyer should check (reglamento, expensas, title of the individual lot).

Output: a table per section with columns Question | Answer | Source + link | Source date | Confidence | Practice vs. law notes. Then a list of the questions you could NOT answer from a primary source.
```
