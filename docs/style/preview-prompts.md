# Claude Design preview prompts

Paste one prompt per session into Claude Design (or `/design` in Claude
Code) with the matching style guide attached. Ask for four artboards:
desktop home (1440 wide, above the fold to the second section), mobile
home (390), one desktop listing card row, and the desktop detail-page
top (gallery + two columns). Do not ask for more; the point is to react
to type and colour in context, not to design the whole site.

## inmobiliaria.com.py

> Design four artboards for a Paraguayan property portal, following the
> attached style guide exactly (palette "Tierra", Lora + Figtree, 4 px
> radius on controls, photo-first listing cards with a cream facts strip
> underneath). Spanish copy, *vos* register, no exclamation marks. Hero:
> full-bleed photo of an ordinary well-kept house with a tile roof, scrim
> from the left, eyebrow "Casas, departamentos y terrenos en todo el
> Paraguay", H1 "Tu próxima casa está más cerca de lo que pensás", a
> search panel (Comprar/Alquilar · Ciudad o barrio · Tipo · Hasta US$ ·
> Buscar) and, beside it, an equally weighted "Publicá gratis" card for
> sellers with a WhatsApp line. Second section: "Recién publicadas", four
> cards with prices like US$ 145.000 and a terracotta cuota line "Cuota
> est. Gs. 4,1 M/mes". Then the trust strip on the soft tint. Mobile:
> sticky bottom bar with WhatsApp and Llamar. Detail page: price in Lora
> 600, specs, description, and a sticky white contact card with the
> WhatsApp button first. Use real Paraguayan place names (Villa Morra,
> Sajonia, Luque, San Lorenzo). No gold, no navy, no uppercase buttons.

## realestateinparaguay.com

> Design four artboards for an English-language guide-and-marketplace
> for foreigners buying property in Paraguay, following the attached
> style guide exactly (palette "Petrol", Newsreader + IBM Plex Sans,
> radius 0, hairline-drawn structure, framed listing cards with the
> price and specs on paper below the photo). Split hero: left paper with
> eyebrow "Property in Paraguay · For international buyers", H1 "Buy
> property in Paraguay. Freehold, in US dollars, from abroad.", a
> three-fact strap, and the search bar; right a photo of Asunción's
> Costanera at dusk. Then a four-cell facts strip (Freehold · USD ·
> ≈ 3–5 % costs · Public deed). Card row: US$ 145,000 · US$ 806/m² on
> line one, "3 bed · 2 bath · 1,938 sq ft" on line two, "Villa Morra,
> Asunción" on line three; no monthly-payment line anywhere. Detail page:
> USD price with a secondary guaraní line, both units, a bordered "Buying
> this property as a foreigner" box, and a sticky contact card with the
> inquiry form first and WhatsApp (+595) second, "We reply in English".
> Include a five-step "How buying works" strip with brass numerals 01–05
> and a small costs table. Tone: broadsheet, factual, no "stunning" or
> "exclusive". No navy, no gold-on-navy, no gradients on text.

## realestateinparaguay.com — variant B, directory-first

Run this as a separate canvas and compare with the guide-first variant
above. Same palette, same type, same components; only the emphasis and
the order change. Both use the attached style guide.

> Design four artboards for an English-language property portal for
> foreigners buying or renting in Paraguay, following the attached style
> guide exactly (palette "Petrol", Newsreader + IBM Plex Sans, radius 0,
> hairline structure, framed listing cards). This variant is
> listings-first: the hero is a full-width photo of Asunción with the
> scrim, eyebrow "Property in Paraguay · For international buyers", H1
> "Homes, apartments and land in Paraguay, priced in US dollars", and a
> large search bar with a Buy / Rent toggle, City, Type, Max price (USD)
> and Search. Directly under the hero a thin one-line facts bar
> (Freehold for foreigners · USD · ≈ 3–5 % closing costs · Public deed),
> each a link, not a big block. Then "New this week" with eight framed
> cards (US$ 145,000 · US$ 806/m² / 3 bed · 2 bath · 1,938 sq ft / Villa
> Morra, Asunción), then "Furnished rentals" with four cards showing
> monthly USD ("US$ 1,200/month · furnished · 12-month min"), then "Where
> to buy" city tiles with median US$/m², and only then a compact
> three-column "How buying works / Costs / Residency" section linking to
> guides. Detail page and mobile as in the guide: no monthly-payment
> line, inquiry form first, WhatsApp +595 second, "We reply in English".
> Tone: factual, no "stunning" or "exclusive". No navy, no gold-on-navy.

How to compare the two: on the guide-first canvas the visitor who has
never heard of Paraguay is reassured before they see a price; on the
directory-first canvas the visitor who already wants a flat in Villa Morra
gets to it one scroll sooner and rentals are visible on the home page.
Pick by which visitor you expect more of in the first year.
