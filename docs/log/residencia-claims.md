# residenciaenparaguay.es — claims to verify (2026-10-01)

The door's copy (`src/content/residency/`) deliberately carries **no price, no
processing time, no income threshold and no statute number**. Every sentence
below is still a factual claim a visitor could act on; the founder (or a
migration lawyer) should confirm each before the site is promoted.

1. Residence is requested from the Dirección Nacional de Migraciones; the
   usual path is *radicación temporal* first, then *permanente* after the
   period the law sets. Some categories may have a more direct route.
2. The documents listed (passport, apostilled criminal record and birth
   certificate, health certificate, proof of means, photos, fees) are the
   typical set; the exact list per category must be checked.
3. Spain and Paraguay are both parties to the Hague Apostille Convention
   (used on the Spain page); Spanish documents need no translation.
4. The Mercosur residence agreement applies to nationals of Argentina,
   Brazil, Uruguay, Bolivia, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador and Peru (the page says
   "confirm your nationality is included"). Spain is not included.
5. Permanent residence is not citizenship; naturalisation is a separate
   process.
6. Foreigners can buy real estate in Paraguay (FAQ on the FAQ page).
7. Cédula for foreigners is issued after the residence is granted; RUC is a
   separate tax registration.
8. Retirees / digital nomads / investors: that the categories exist and what
   proof they need is described generically; no minimum income or investment
   is quoted. Add the real figures only after verifying them.
9. Business claims we made on the site: "acompañamiento" (assistance) with
   the whole procedure, and that costs are split into official fees vs
   service. No price and no free-consultation promise anywhere.
