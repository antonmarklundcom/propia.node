## Body copy (reading order)

From luxury residential acquisitions to high-yield commercial portfolios. We provide the bilingual expertise and local intelligence required for secure, world-class property transactions.

Asunción is currently undergoing an unprecedented urban transformation. With the rise of the New Financial Center and the luxury corridors of Santa Teresa and Villa Morra, the city has become a magnet for international capital. However, navigating this market as an outsider requires more than just access to listings—it requires a deep understanding of local appreciation trends, neighborhood dynamics, and the “off-market” network that defines Paraguayan real estate.

As your dedicated Realtors in Asunción, we act as your fiduciary representatives. We don’t just sell property; we curate opportunities. Whether you are seeking a primary residence that reflects your status or a diversified portfolio of apartments to maximize rental yields, we provide the data-driven insights and bilingual negotiation power to ensure you never overpay and always buy with an exit strategy in mind.

We go beyond the public portals. Our team leverages a private network of developers and local owners to find "silent listings" and pre-construction opportunities that are not yet available to the general market. We filter every option based on construction quality, developer reputation, and long-term resale potential.

A safe purchase is a successful purchase. We coordinate with top-tier notaries (Escribanos) and legal experts to perform exhaustive title searches, ensuring the property is 100% free of liens or legal encumbrances. We provide full English translations of all legal documents so you move forward with absolute clarity.

Negotiating in a foreign culture can be daunting. We act as your advocate, utilizing local market data to secure the best possible purchase price and terms. Our goal is to eliminate the "foreigner premium" and ensure that your investment starts with equity on day one.

Our relationship doesn't end at the notary's office. Once you acquire your property, we assist with the seamless transition of titles, utility setups, and if needed, the immediate integration into our Apartment Management or Airbnb Management systems to begin generating returns instantly.

Access the best "floor-one" prices in new developments with our vetted list of top-tier developers.

Tailored searches for high-security villas and penthouses in the city's most prestigious gated communities.

Identifying strategic plots for future development or commercial use in emerging industrial zones.

In a market as dynamic as Asunción, professional representation is the difference between a speculative gamble and a calculated investment.

The primary benefit of working with a professional Realtor is the mitigation of risk. We vet the developers behind the projects, analyze the quality of materials used in construction, and evaluate the future infrastructure plans for the area. This ensures that your capital is placed in assets that will appreciate over time, protected from market volatility.

For the international investor or expat, time is the most valuable commodity. We streamline the entire acquisition process—from initial scouting to final deed registration. By acting as your single point of contact, we eliminate the need for you to coordinate with multiple agents, lawyers, and government offices, providing a true “turn-key” brokerage experience.

Yes. Paraguay is very open to foreign investment. You can purchase and title property under your own name or a corporation using only your valid passport.

Typically, closing costs (including notary fees and taxes) range between 2% and 3% of the transaction value. We provide a detailed "closing statement" estimate before any deal is finalized.

Paraguay offers a very competitive tax environment. If you sell as an individual, the tax on the gain is usually quite low (effectively around 2.4% to 3% in many cases), making it an excellent place for capital appreciation.

Whether you are looking for a luxury home, a strategic residency, or high-yield investment opportunities in Asunción, our team is ready to provide the local expertise you need.
