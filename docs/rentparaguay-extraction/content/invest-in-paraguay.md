Capitalize on South America’s most competitive tax regime and a booming real estate market. From high-yield residential developments to large-scale agribusiness, we are your strategic partner in Paraguay.

Paraguay has officially entered a new era. Having recently achieved Investment Grade status, the country has solidified its position as a safe haven for international capital. With a consistent GDP growth that outpaces the regional average and a currency (the Guaraní) that remains one of the most stable in Latin America, the “hidden gem” of the south is now a global priority.

The secret to Paraguay’s success lies in its fiscal discipline and the “10-10-10” system: 10% corporate tax, 10% personal income tax, and 10% VAT. Furthermore, under the new Law No. 7548/2025, international investors benefit from even deeper incentives, including total exemptions on dividends and profit remittances for high-impact projects. Whether you are looking for capital preservation or aggressive growth, the macro-fundamentals of Paraguay provide a foundation of security that is rare in today’s global market.

We identify "Alpha" opportunities in Asunción’s premium corridors like Santa Teresa and Villa Morra. Our team focuses on high-liquidity assets—such as luxury residential towers and corporate office spaces—that offer rental yields between 6% and 10% and annual appreciation rates of 7% to 9%. We handle the sourcing, acquisition, and management to ensure your portfolio outperforms the market.

Paraguay is a global powerhouse in food production. We facilitate investments in high-fertility land for soybean, corn, and livestock operations. With farmland prices significantly lower than in neighboring Brazil or Argentina, and a global demand for beef and grains at an all-time high, we provide the local expertise to acquire and manage productive land with projected IRRs exceeding 10%.

Maximize your returns through professional legal engineering. Our legal partners ensure your investment is structured to benefit from Paraguay’s territorial tax system and international investment protection treaties.

For the absent investor, we provide a complete "boots on the ground" solution. You receive transparent, bilingual reporting every quarter, allowing you to monitor your South American portfolio from anywhere in the world with total confidence.

Investing in Paraguay is a move toward efficiency. The combination of low operational costs, the cheapest renewable energy in the region (Itaipú), and a pro-business government makes it the most logical choice for capital looking for a competitive edge.

Paraguay’s territorial tax system is a magnet for global citizens. You are only taxed on income generated within the territory, meaning your worldwide wealth remains untouched. Additionally, the absence of inheritance, wealth, or gift taxes makes it an ideal jurisdiction for long-term estate planning and generational wealth preservation.

Strategically located in the heart of the continent, Paraguay serves as a low-cost manufacturing and logistics hub for a market of over 270 million people. The Maquila Law allows companies to import raw materials duty-free and export finished goods with a symbolic 1% tax, providing a back-door entrance to the massive economies of Brazil and Argentina.
