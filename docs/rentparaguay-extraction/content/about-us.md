## Body copy (reading order)

Rent Paraguay was founded on the principle that international mobility should be seamless. We noticed a significant gap in the market: global investors and expats were arriving in a booming Paraguay but struggling with local bureaucracy, inconsistent service standards, and a lack of bilingual transparency.

We built this firm to be the “Soft Landing” partner. We combine Swedish efficiency—focused on honesty, punctuality, and high-quality standards—with a deep, boots-on-the-ground expertise in the Paraguayan legal and real estate landscape.

Rent Paraguay is a boutique consultancy firm that serves as the premier gateway for international investors, expats, and digital nomads seeking a high-quality life in the heart of South America. Founded by Anton Marklund, a Swedish entrepreneur who relocated to Asunción three years ago. We don't just find properties; we engineer "soft landings."

To become the most trusted and aesthetically sophisticated bridge for global capital and talent in Paraguay, setting the gold standard for transparency, design-led real estate, and seamless relocation.

To eliminate the friction of international transitions by providing world-class bilingual advisory, impeccable property management, and strategic investment guidance, all delivered with Scandinavian precision and local soul.
