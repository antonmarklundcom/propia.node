## Body copy (reading order)

We protect your real estate investment in Asunción with rigorous tenant screening, proactive maintenance, and transparent financial reporting.

Owning a long-term rental in a growing market like Asunción is an excellent financial strategy, but it often comes with hidden operational costs that can erode your returns. For many owners—especially those living abroad—the stress of self-management leads to “landlord burnout”. From chasing late payments to navigating the local legal system, the administrative burden can be overwhelming.

Our mission is to turn your property into a truly passive income stream. We believe that professional management is not just about collecting rent; it’s about preserving the physical integrity of your asset and fostering positive, long-term relationships with quality tenants. By delegating the day-to-day operations to us, you gain the freedom to focus on your next investment while we handle the complexities of the Paraguayan market.

Proactive Maintenance & PreservationThe foundation of a successful rental is a high-quality tenant. We don’t rely on gut feelings; we perform deep background, financial, and reference checks to ensure only the most reliable individuals occupy your property. Our marketing team uses high-impact listings and professional photography to find these qualified tenants fast, minimizing the costly “vacancy days” that impact your bottom line.

A property that isn’t cared for is a liability. We provide 24/7 proactive maintenance through our network of trusted local contractors who handle repairs quickly and at fair market prices. By catching small issues like leaks or electrical faults early, we prevent them from turning into expensive structural repairs, effectively preserving your property’s long-term resale value.

Navigating Paraguayan rental laws and contract renewals requires specific expertise. We handle all lease drafting and legal notifications according to current regulations, ensuring your interests are always protected. Every month, you will receive a detailed financial statement in English, providing total transparency on your investment’s performance.

We begin by creating a professional profile for your property, utilizing high-impact listings and persuasive descriptions to attract a premium audience. We personally escort potential tenants during showings and conduct a deep-dive financial analysis of their income and stability to guarantee a secure, long-term match for your asset.

Legal safety is our top priority. We coordinate the signing of all lease documents at a Notary Public (Escribanía) to ensure maximum legal weight. Furthermore, we manage security deposits in secure accounts and produce detailed photo-documented inventory reports to prevent future disputes and protect your property's condition.

Once the tenant moves in, we act as the sole point of contact. We provide 24/7 proactive maintenance through our network of trusted local contractors, resolving issues quickly and at fair market prices. Our team conducts regular inspections to ensure the tenant is caring for your home according to our strict quality standards.

You stay in control without the headache. Every month, you will receive a comprehensive financial statement in English, detailing income, expenses, and net profit. We also assist in managing local property taxes and building expenses (expensas), ensuring your investment remains compliant and profitable year-round.

Using professional property management offers a range of benefits that help owners—whether individuals or corporations—make better, safer, and more profitable property returns while maintaining total peace of mind.
